{bold}About {board}{/}

This board is a folder. board.toml says its menus and its doors, and
each screen is a text file in screens/, with marks for {{colors}},
{{sounds}} and {{effects}}, and a line of its own for each picture or
turning model.

Callers in Flow see the pictures and models and hear the sounds;
callers in other terminals see the words in their own colors.
