{bold}{bright-cyan}Demonstrations{/}{fx ripple}

 What a board can show in {bold}Flow{/}, the terminal: pictures, models that
 turn, effects on the glass, sounds, and doors that draw for themselves.

 {bold}Neko{/} is the desktop cat of 1989. Move your mouse and it runs to the
 pointer; leave it be and it washes, yawns and falls asleep. Where there's
 no mouse, the arrows move a mark for it to chase. {bold}q{/} or {bold}Esc{/} brings you back.

 {dim}In another terminal Neko is a cat in text, and the gallery names what it
 would show. Dial the board from Flow to see them as they are.{/}
