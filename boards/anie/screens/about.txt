{bold}{bright-cyan}About {board}{/}

 A bulletin board, as they used to be, for Anie Solutions: forums to talk
 in, and a bug tracker to tell us what's broken in what we make.

 {bold}Membership{/}
 Apply from the welcome: a handle, your name, your email address (a code
 comes to it), a password, and an authenticator app on your phone
 (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy...).
 The sysop reads each application and lets members in.

 {bold}Logging in{/}
 Your handle, your password, and the 6-digit code your app shows. Keep
 the recovery codes you're given: each gets you in once if the phone is
 lost. Forgotten your password? A code to your email sets a new one.

 {bold}Roles{/}
 {bright-white}member{/}   reads and posts in the forums, files and comments on bugs
 {bright-white}staff{/}    keeps the forums tidy and triages bugs
 {bright-white}sysop{/}    runs the board

 {dim}Times on the board are Tokyo time. Best in Flow (dial it from Flow's
 phonebook); any terminal's ssh works too.{/}
