{bold}{bright-cyan}The rules of {board}{/}

 1. Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people.
 2. No spam, no advertising, nothing illegal.
 3. Keep customers' and colleagues' private details private: no
    passwords, keys, personal data or anyone's confidential work.
 4. File bugs with what you did, what you expected and what happened.
    One bug a report.
 5. One account a person. Handles are for the board; your real name
    and email are seen by the sysop alone.
 6. The sysop and staff may remove posts and suspend members who
    break these rules.

 {dim}What you post is kept on Anie Solutions' server. Ask the sysop to
 close your account and have your details removed.{/}
