# Neko's sprites

The PNGs in this folder are Neko's: the cat that chases the mouse pointer,
first written by Naoshi Watanabe for the NEC PC-9801 and drawn again by
Kenji Gotoh for NekoDA, his desk accessory for the Macintosh, in 1989.
[Wikipedia's article on Neko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_(software))
says the NekoDA sprites made by Gotoh are free to use, modify and
redistribute, as is the code of many of the ports since.

They were taken as 32 × 32 PNGs from
[crgimenes/neko](https://github.com/crgimenes/neko) (whose own code is
under the BSD 2-Clause licence, and whose README says it uses the
original's sprites and sounds and none of its code): 32 of its 40 frames,
its footprints left out. flow-cat draws them bigger pixel by pixel and
moves them with its own code; the sprites themselves are as they came.

They're carried here under those terms, not under Flow-BBS's own MIT or
Apache-2.0 licence, which covers the rest of this repository.
